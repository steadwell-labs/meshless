# Code of conduct

## The short version

Argue about the protocol, not about the person. Be precise, be generous with
context, and assume the other participant is trying to make the same thing work.

## Expected

- Critique ideas with reasons: a failure case, an interoperability cost, a
  threat, a citation.
- Say "I don't know" and "you may be right" when they are true.
- Keep review comments about the artifact. "This binding can be replayed when
  the clock is skewed" is a review. "This is amateur" is not.
- Respect that maintainers say no to changes that carry protocol cost, without
  interpreting that as a judgement of your work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or sustained condescension.
- Discriminatory comments about any person or group.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Repeatedly relitigating a settled decision outside the proposal process.
- Using the project's channels to promote a product, token, or unrelated cause.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, project chat, and any space where you are
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers at Steadwell Labs. Reports are handled
privately. Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, proportional
to the behaviour and its repetition. Maintainers recuse themselves from
decisions about their own conduct.

This policy is deliberately short. Where it is silent, apply the norm: technical
rigour is expected, contempt is not.
